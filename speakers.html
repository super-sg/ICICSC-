Proposals

Propose a keynote or an industry talk

We are actively seeking speakers whose work would extend the conversation beyond the accepted papers — particularly researchers working at the boundary between two of our tracks, and practitioners who can speak candidly about what fails in deployment.

Send a short proposal — a working title, a paragraph of abstract, a two-line biography and a link to your publication record — to the programme chairs. Proposals are reviewed fortnightly.
