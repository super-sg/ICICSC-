What registration includes

  • Conference kit, delegate badge and programme booklet
  • Access to all keynote, plenary and technical sessions
  • Pre-conference tutorials on 16 June 2027
  • Lunch and refreshments on all three days
  • Conference banquet

Policies

Cancellation and refunds

Registration is transferable to a co-author but is non-refundable once a paper has been accepted, because the fee covers publication processing. Attendee registrations cancelled in writing before 1 June 2027 are refunded at 50%; no refund is made after that date.

Substitution

A registered delegate may nominate a substitute in writing up to seven days before the conference at no extra charge.

No-show

A paper that is neither presented on-site nor online is withdrawn from the proceedings, and the fee is not refunded.

Receipts and GST

Official receipts are issued by the university finance office. Delegates needing a GST invoice must supply their GSTIN on the registration form.

Concessions

Requests for a concessional rate on grounds of financial hardship are considered confidentially. Write to the organising secretariat.

Registration questions

Frequently asked

When is the last date to register?

Authors of accepted papers must register by 20 May 2027 to benefit from the early-bird rate, and no later than 1 June 2027 in any case. A paper whose author has not registered by 1 June 2027 is dropped from the programme and the proceedings.

Do co-authors need to register separately?

Only one author must register for the paper to be published and presented. Co-authors who wish to attend the conference must register separately at the applicable rate, and receive their own conference kit, meals and certificate.

What proof do students need?

A scanned copy of a valid institutional identity card, or a bonafide certificate signed by the head of department, dated within the current academic year. Upload it with the registration form.

Can my institution pay directly?

Yes. Institutional transfers are accepted. Ask your finance office to quote the delegate's name and paper ID in the transfer narration, and email a copy of the remittance advice to the secretariat.

I registered but my payment is not reflected.

Bank reconciliation can take up to five working days. If your confirmation has not arrived after that, write to registration@icngci2027.sharda.ac.in with your transaction reference, the date of payment and your paper ID.

Registration closes 1 June 2027

Early-bird rates end on 20 May 2027. Register early to secure the lower fee.
