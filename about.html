Overview

About ICNGCI 2027

The International Conference on Next-Generation Computing and Innovations (ICNGCI 2027) is a three-day international forum hosted by Sharda University, Greater Noida, from 17 to 19 June 2027.

ICNGCI exists to close the distance between computing research and the problems it is meant to solve — gathering researchers, doctoral scholars, engineers and industry experts to present original work and stress-test ideas in open discussion.

Six tracks cover ninety topic areas, from the foundations of machine learning and post-quantum cryptography to satellite networking, digital twins and technology governance. Work that sits deliberately between tracks is equally welcome.

Every submission receives at least three independent double-blind reviews from a standing panel of over 260 reviewers. Accepted papers are presented alongside plenary keynotes, industry talks, a doctoral symposium, tutorials and a poster session, with best paper, best student paper and best presentation awards in each track.

The conference runs in hybrid mode — authors who cannot travel present remotely under the same review standard, with no difference in how their paper is treated.

Objectives

What the conference sets out to do

01

Convene the field

Bring together researchers, scientists, engineers, policymakers and industry experts to present and debate innovative computing solutions to pressing technical and societal problems.

02

Cross disciplinary lines

Encourage collaboration between computing and healthcare, environmental science, agriculture, manufacturing, mobility, economics, education and public policy.

03

Showcase advances

Provide a rigorously reviewed venue for advances in artificial intelligence, data science, cybersecurity, next-generation networks, IoT and cyber-physical systems.

04

Develop early researchers

Give research scholars and postgraduate students a structured platform — the doctoral symposium and tutorial track — to present work in progress and receive senior mentorship.

05

Connect academia and industry

Create a working channel between laboratories and practice through practitioner talks, a start-up showcase, exhibition space and a recruitment corner.

06

Align with sustainable development

Prioritise contributions that advance the UN Sustainable Development Goals, in energy, health, water, climate, inclusive infrastructure and responsible technology.

Proceedings

Publication and indexing

Selected peer-reviewed papers are proposed for publication in the conference proceedings, published with Springer.

The publisher conducts its own quality checks on accepted papers, and only papers that pass those checks are published. Springer Nature does not charge any fee for the publication of non-open-access content. Abstracts, extended abstracts and short papers under four pages are not considered for publication in the proceedings volume.

All communication regarding the proceedings must be directed to the conference organisers or the volume editors. Authors should not contact the publisher directly. Detailed instructions for authors and editors of conference proceedings are published by the publisher at springer.com/gp/authors-editors/conference-proceedings.

Author guidelines Call for papers

Conditions for inclusion

  • The paper passes double-blind peer review.
  • Text similarity is below the declared threshold, excluding references.
  • At least one author registers at the applicable rate.
  • The paper is presented at the conference, on-site or online.
  • A signed copyright form and a camera-ready file in the publisher's template are submitted by the deadline.

A paper that is accepted but not presented is withdrawn from the proceedings.

Host institution

Sharda University, Greater Noida

Sharda University is a leading educational institution based at Greater Noida (NCR-Delhi), India — a venture of the Sharda Group of Institutions.

The University has established itself as a high-quality education provider with a prime focus on holistic learning and on building competitive ability in its students. It is approved by the University Grants Commission and is the only multi-discipline campus in the National Capital Region, spread over 63 acres and equipped with world-class facilities.

Sharda University has been accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an A+ grade, and was ranked 87th in the University category in the NIRF 2023 Rankings. The campus hosts students from more than 95 countries alongside Sharda Hospital, a 1,600-bed multi-speciality teaching hospital.

ICNGCI 2027 is organised by the Sharda School of Computing Science & Engineering, which runs undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across computer science, computer applications and allied disciplines, and whose faculty work in artificial intelligence, wireless and sensor networks, security, IoT and biomedical computing. The school has hosted a sustained series of international conferences with indexed proceedings.

Recognition

Awards

Best Paper Award

One award per track, decided by an independent panel on the basis of review scores, technical novelty and the quality of the presentation. Carries a certificate and a citation.

Best Student Paper Award

Open to papers whose first author is a registered postgraduate or doctoral student and who presents the work in person or online.

Best Presentation Award

Awarded in every technical session by the session chairs, recognising clarity of delivery and the quality of engagement with the audience.

Policy

Research ethics and code of conduct

ICNGCI is committed to a professional, respectful and harassment-free environment for every participant, regardless of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion or career stage.

Plagiarism and originality

Authors must submit original and unpublished work that is not under consideration elsewhere. Every submission is screened for text similarity. Papers found to be plagiarised at any stage — before review, during review, or after acceptance — are rejected, and the authors' institutions are informed. Self-plagiarism and redundant publication are treated with the same seriousness.

Use of generative AI

Generative AI tools may be used for language editing and for improving readability, and this use should be disclosed in the acknowledgements. AI systems cannot be listed as authors. Authors remain fully responsible for the accuracy, originality and integrity of every part of the submitted work, including any text, code, figures or references produced with assistance from such tools. Undisclosed, substantive use of generative AI to produce research content is treated as research misconduct.

Authorship and conflicts of interest

All listed authors must have made a substantive intellectual contribution and must agree to the submission. The author list cannot be changed after acceptance. Reviewers and programme committee members must declare conflicts of interest and are excluded from handling papers by close collaborators, students, supervisors or colleagues at the same institution.

Human subjects, data and reproducibility

Work involving human participants or personally identifiable data must state its ethical approval and its consent procedure. Authors are strongly encouraged to release code, datasets and experimental configurations so that results can be reproduced, and to state clearly any restriction that prevents them from doing so.

Code of conduct at the event

Harassment of any kind, including unwelcome attention, intimidation, sustained disruption of sessions and inappropriate photography or recording, is not tolerated. Participants asked to stop such behaviour must comply immediately. Concerns may be raised in confidence with any member of the organising committee or by writing to the conference secretariat, and will be handled promptly and discreetly.
