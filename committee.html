More reviewers welcome

The reviewer panel above continues to grow. Members are published here, with their affiliations, once invitations are confirmed. Every paper receives at least three independent double-blind reviews from committee members with no conflict of interest.

If you hold a doctorate, or are a doctoral candidate with publications in the conference areas, we would value your help. Reviewers handle three to five papers between 20 April and 30 April 2027 and are acknowledged by name in the proceedings.

Volunteer as a reviewer

What we ask of reviewers

  • Declare every conflict of interest before accepting a paper.
  • Return reviews by the stated date, or decline early so the paper can be reassigned.
  • Judge the work, not the authors — review is double-blind in both directions.
  • Give reasons an author can act on, not a verdict alone.
  • Treat every submission as confidential and never reuse its content.
  • Do not submit any part of a paper under review to a generative AI service.
