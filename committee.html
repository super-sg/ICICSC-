Call for reviewers

ICNGCI depends on the volunteered time of researchers willing to review carefully and on schedule. Reviewers handle three to five papers between 20 April and 30 April 2027 and are acknowledged by name in the proceedings.

  • Declare every conflict of interest before accepting a paper.
  • Return reviews by the stated date, or decline early so the paper can be reassigned.
  • Judge the work, not the authors — review is double-blind in both directions.
  • Give reasons an author can act on, not a verdict alone.
  • Treat every submission as confidential and never reuse its content.
  • Do not submit any part of a paper under review to a generative AI service.
