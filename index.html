About the conference

A forum where next-generation computing meets real-world impact

ICNGCI 2027 brings together researchers, doctoral scholars, practising engineers and industry experts to present original work and debate where computing goes next.

Six technical tracks span AI, data science, cybersecurity and blockchain, next-generation networks, IoT and robotics, and interdisciplinary computing. Every submission gets at least three independent double-blind reviews, and accepted papers are presented alongside keynotes, industry talks, a doctoral symposium and hands-on tutorials.

Work connecting computing to healthcare, sustainability, agriculture, manufacturing, mobility or public policy is especially welcome.

Publishing & indexing

All accepted and presented papers are compiled into the conference proceedings. Selected peer-reviewed papers are proposed for publication with Springer, our publication partner, subject to the publisher's own quality checks.

  • Papers shorter than four pages, abstracts and extended abstracts are not considered for publication.
  • No publication charge is levied by the publisher for non-open-access content.

Venue

Hosted on campus, connected to the world

Sharda University, Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida — the only multi-discipline campus in the Delhi NCR, spread over 63 acres.

The conference occupies the auditorium and seminar halls of the Sharda School of Computing Science & Engineering. Guest-house rooms and partner hotels at negotiated rates are available, and an airport shuttle runs on all conference days.
