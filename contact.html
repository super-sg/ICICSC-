Quick answers

Before you write

When will I hear about my paper?

Decisions are released to the corresponding author on 5 May 2027, with all reviewer comments. Please do not ask for a status update before that date — every paper is decided together.

Can I get a certificate of participation?

Yes. Every registered delegate receives a certificate. Presenting authors receive a certificate of presentation naming the paper. Certificates are issued at the venue and emailed as PDFs afterwards.

Will the proceedings be Scopus indexed?

Selected papers are proposed for publication with Springer. Indexing is decided by the indexing service, not by the conference or the publisher, and no conference anywhere can guarantee it in advance. We publish the confirmed publication arrangement on the about page as soon as it is signed.

Is there a discount for a group from one institution?

Groups of five or more delegates from the same institution registering together may request a group rate. Write to the registration address with the list of names and paper IDs.

Can I attend without presenting?

Yes. Register in the attendee category. Attendees have access to all sessions, tutorials and meals, and receive a certificate of participation.

Do you offer press accreditation?

Yes, for accredited journalists and science communicators. Write to the general enquiries address with your outlet and editor's contact.
